<h1>Child Safety Standards – Chat Alkhaleej</h1>

<p>Chat Alkhaleej is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).</p>

<h2>Zero Tolerance Policy</h2>

<p>We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any content, behavior, or activity that promotes, depicts, facilitates, or encourages child sexual abuse or exploitation.</p>

<h2>Prohibited Content</h2>

<ul>
<li>Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)</li>
<li>Sexual exploitation of minors</li>
<li>Grooming or predatory behavior</li>
<li>Solicitation involving minors</li>
<li>Any content that endangers children</li>
<li>Sharing, requesting, or distributing inappropriate images involving minors</li>
</ul>

<h2>Safety Measures</h2>

<ul>
<li>Users can report inappropriate content and accounts.</li>
<li>Users can block other users.</li>
<li>Reported content is reviewed and moderated.</li>
<li>Violating accounts may be suspended or permanently banned.</li>
<li>We cooperate with relevant legal authorities when required by law.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Reporting Concerns</h2>

<p>If you encounter content or behavior that may threaten child safety, please report it immediately through the application or contact us directly.</p>
