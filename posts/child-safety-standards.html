Child Safety Standards – Chat Alkhaleej

Chat Alkhaleej is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero Tolerance Policy

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any content, behavior, or activity that promotes, depicts, facilitates, or encourages child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited Content

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Grooming or predatory behavior
  • Solicitation involving minors
  • Any content that endangers children
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing inappropriate images involving minors

Safety Measures

  • Users can report inappropriate content and accounts.
  • Users can block other users.
  • Reported content is reviewed and moderated.
  • Violating accounts may be suspended or permanently banned.
  • We cooperate with relevant legal authorities when required by law.

Reporting Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior that may threaten child safety, please report it immediately through the application or contact us directly.
